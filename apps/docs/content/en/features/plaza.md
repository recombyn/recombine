# Plaza & inspiration

Browse **official cases** and community work; open a copy in your editor.

## Browse

- Home / Plaza: **Recommended / Latest / Following** (labels may vary).
- Categories: website, mobile, image, poster, illustration, etc.
- **Official cases**: curated templates / samples (resume, poster, dashboard, …).
- Opening a case copies it into your project for manual or Agent edits.

## Publish to Plaza

1. Prepare a cover artboard (multi-frame: pick a representative page).
2. Submit title and details.
3. **Admin review** required before public listing.

No infringing or prohibited content ([Terms](/legal/terms)). Revise and resubmit if rejected.

## Profile

| Tab | Notes |
|-----|-------|
| **Published** | Your approved Plaza posts |
| **Liked** | Items you liked |
| **Assets** | Related assets (per product) |

Profile is separate from the editor project library.

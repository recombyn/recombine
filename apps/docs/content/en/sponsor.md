# Sponsor recombyn

Hi — I’m the solo developer behind recombyn.

I used to write software for a living. No impressive degree on paper, and for years I mostly bounced through outsourcing gigs — projects follow the client, and the work is rarely stable. Then came a layoff (one of those rounds that feels a lot like “AI is rearranging the deck”). Unemployed, applications went nowhere: in this era, even if you can code, landing a job is hard. I didn’t know what to do next, so I built something I actually wanted to exist: recombyn — editable design on an infinite canvas, driven by conversation.

No team. No fundraising pitch deck. Nights and weekends, learning as I ship. The product still has plenty of gaps — stability, polish, docs, and a clear commercial path. Fundraising felt heavy; turning this into a “proper” SaaS means licenses, certificates, and compliance costs that are hard for one person to carry.

There’s no paywall here and no “sponsor or else.”  
If the project helped you — or you simply want to support an indie developer who’s still grinding — **voluntary support** means a lot. Every yuan goes toward servers, model usage, and making the product a bit more solid.

Thank you for reading this far.

## Community

Sponsorship isn’t buying a feature pack. It helps this small project last longer: fix bugs, ship capabilities, and respond to feedback. Using it, sharing it, or opening an issue helps too.
